  • Motivational (Age Group + Single Age) — USA Swimming, 2024-2028 National Age Group / Single Age Motivational Time Standards, official PDF revised 2025-10-10. Links: USA Swimming official standards page · SCY standards PDF · SwimStandards cross-check
  • EZ Age Group Champs (SC / LC) — Eastern Zone Swimming, 2026 Eastern Zone Age Group Championships Qualifying Times, official QT tables. Links: Short-course meet announcement · Long-course meet announcement
  • EZ Sectionals — Eastern Zone Swimming, 2027 Eastern Zone Speedo Sectional Qualifying Times — one table shared by the three Mar 18–21, 2027 meets (Buffalo LC / Providence SC North / Christiansburg SC South); the 50 BK/BR/FL now have their own standards. Link: Official standards PDF
  • NCSA Age Group — 2026 NCSA Age Group Swimming Championships meet announcement (Orlando, Mar 25-28, 2026); NCSA has announced the standards are unchanged for 2027. Links: Standards PDF · SwimStandards cross-check
  • NCSA Juniors (Spring / Summer) — 2026 NCSA Spring Swimming Championships Time Standards and the 2026 NCSA Summer Swimming Championships meet announcement (18&U). Links: Spring standards PDF · Summer meet announcement PDF
  • Futures — USA Swimming, 2027 TYR Futures Championships Time Standards (Jul 28–31, 2027; Carmel / Cary / Minneapolis / Ocala / Sacramento), 18&U table — identical to the 2026 table, re-issued for 2027. Link: Official standards PDF
  • Winter Juniors — USA Swimming, 2026 Speedo Winter Junior Championships time standards (East, Indianapolis, Dec 9-12, 2026). Link: USA Swimming official standards page (2026 standards documents)
  • Summer Juniors — USA Swimming, 2027 Speedo Junior National Championships Time Standards (Huntsville, AL, Aug 2–6, 2027), QT only. Link: Official standards PDF
  • U.S. Nationals — USA Swimming, 2026 Toyota U.S. National Championships Time Standards (Irvine, Jul 28–Aug 1, 2026). Published as two age tables — 18 & Under and 19 & Over. The app shows the table for the swimmer's age: 18 & Under swimmers see the 18&U table, swimmers 19+ see the 19 & Over table. Age note: entry and these standards tables use the swimmer's age on Day 1 of the meet; age during the event — including eligibility for its 18-U Final — is World Aquatics age (as of December 31, 2026). The 2027 edition (Indianapolis, Jun 8–12, 2027) has been announced but its standards are not yet published, so the 2026 set is used. Link: Official standards PDF
  • U.S. Open — USA Swimming, 2026 Toyota U.S. Open Time Standards (Austin, Dec 2–5, 2026), primary standards table. Link: Official standards PDF
  • Olympic Trials — USA Swimming, 2028 U.S. Olympic Team Trials – Swimming Qualifying Standards (Indianapolis, Jun 9–17, 2028), announced Oct 15, 2026; LCM only, single tier. Links: Official standards PDF · USA Swimming announcement
  • TYR Pro Swim Series — USA Swimming, 2027 TYR Pro Swim Series Time Standards. New for 2027: the series publishes two tables — Nashville (Jan 14–17) & Westmont (Mar 3–6) share one, Fort Lauderdale (Apr 21–24) has its own, slightly faster table; each comes in 19 & Over and 18 & Under age tables, and the app shows the one for the swimmer's age. No usaswimming.org-hosted copy of the 2027 posters could be located; these are the official posters as distributed to clubs. Links: Nashville & Westmont poster (PDF) · Fort Lauderdale poster (PDF)
  • Open Water Nationals / Jr Nationals — USA Swimming, 2027 Open Water National & Junior National Championships (Sarasota, FL, Apr 2–4, 2027). The ladder shows the pool-time standards, which key on distance freestyle only (800/1500 LCM, 1000/1650 SCY) and qualify you for the 10K or the 3K Knockout & 5K as labeled; the 5K and 3K Knockout can also be reached by placement at Zone Open Water Championships (see the Additional Criteria). There is no 7.5K in 2027. Links: Official standards PDF · 2027 Additional Criteria (PDF)
  • ISCA Championships — International Swim Coaches Association (St. Petersburg, FL). Four meets: International Senior Cup (2027; one Senior table, SCY/LCM — entry requires age 12+ on day 1, so it appears from age 12) and East Coast Elite Showcase (2027; single-age tables 9–14, SCY/SCM/LCM — the age-9 table is the meet's 9 & Under group; its 100 IM has no LCM standard), plus the summer pair in their 2026 editions (the 2027 summer standards are not yet published): Summer Elite Showcase (single-age 9–14, SCY/LCM) and Summer Senior Blast (one Senior table, all three courses). ISCA's bonus swims are an entry mechanism (qualify in one event, enter bonus events) — no bonus time standards are published, so nothing is excluded here. Links: Senior Cup 2027 meet info (PDF) · East Elite Showcase 2027 (PDF) · Summer Elite Showcase 2026 (PDF) · Summer Senior Blast 2026 (PDF)
Local LSC references
  • MD Champs / MD Jr Champs — Maryland Swimming, 2026 Maryland LSC Championships Qualifying Times and the 2026 Junior Championships meet announcements (Site 1 / Site 2, Mar 2026). Links: SCY Champs standards · LCM standards · Jr Champs / LCQ document
  • PVS 14&U Champs — Potomac Valley Swimming (pick PVS under "Your LSC"). Two meets, one table each with SCY + LCM qualifying times: SC Championships 2027 (College Park, Mar 11–14, 2027) and LC Championships 2026 (Jul 16–20, 2026; the 2027 LC standards were not yet published). The LC table's 13-14 50 BK/BR/FL are not separate cuts — you qualify via the 100, as published. Links: SC 2027 standards (PDF) · LC 2026 standards (PDF)
  • PVS Open Champs — Potomac Valley Swimming. SC Championships 2027: one Open table. LC Championships 2026: Junior and Senior divisions of one meet — the Junior division is a band (make the Junior cut, but be no faster than the Senior cut), so the ladder shows the Junior cut as an upper-bound rung next to the Senior cut. Links: SC 2027 standards (PDF) · LC 2026 standards (PDF) · PVS qualifying-times index
  • VA Champs — Virginia Swimming (pick VA under "Your LSC"). Quadrennial tables, 2025–2028, one set serving both the SC and LC championship meets, with SCY + SCM + LCM columns: Age Group Champs (10U / 11-12 / 13-14) and Senior Champs. The 12 District Championship meets publish no table of their own — they key off these same Age Group marks. Links: Age Group Champs standards (PDF) · Senior Champs standards (PDF)
  • MA Champs (10&U / Junior / Senior / Silver) — Middle Atlantic Swimming, 2027 Championship Qualifying Standards (pick MA under "Your LSC"). One table per meet with SCY + SCM + LCM columns. Silver Champs is a band: make the Silver standard, but be no faster than the Junior/Senior cut — that cut is the one that counts. Note: the Senior women's 50 Back SCM standard is printed as 27.39, faster than its own SCY mark — shown exactly as published. Links: MA time standards index · 10&U standards (PDF) · Junior standards (PDF) · Senior standards (PDF) · Silver standards (PDF)
  • MR Champs (Silver / Junior Mets / AGTC / Senior Mets) — Metropolitan Swimming (pick MR under "Your LSC"). The short-course age-group standards live in one single-age chart (Silver, Junior Mets and the 15-18 AGTC columns side by side). That chart is the 2022–2023 printing — still the standing chart linked for the current season; no newer edition has been issued. AGTC is a band: make the AGTC Min, but be no faster than the AGTC Max, which sits at the Senior Mets cut. Bronze Champs has no floor — its ceiling is the Silver column itself (slower than Silver = Bronze-eligible), so it is not a separate rung. Senior Mets (2026) uses one table for the Winter and Summer meets; its 50 BK/BR/FL have no standard of their own (you qualify via the 100). Junior Mets Summer (2026) adds the long-course season, ages 9&U–15-18. Links: SC age-group chart (PDF) · Senior Mets Winter 2026 (PDF) · Senior Mets Summer 2026 (PDF) · Junior Mets Summer 2026 (PDF)
  • NI Champs (Gold / Silver) — Niagara Swimming (pick NI under "Your LSC"), 2025–2026 Championship Time Standards — the current edition; the LSC's standards page lists the 2026–2027 set as "coming soon". One Gold table serves the 14&U Gold Championships, the Open Gold Championships and the LC Championships (SCY + SCM + LCM columns). Silver Champs is a band: make the Silver standard, but be slower than the Gold standard (one exception as published: for the 10&U 500 Free the ceiling is the 11-12 Gold standard). Bronze Champs has no floor — its ceiling is the Silver table itself — so it is not a separate rung. Links: NI time standards index · Gold standards (PDF) · Silver standards (PDF) · Silvers meet announcement (band rule)
  • AD Champs (Gold / LC) — Adirondack Swimming (pick AD under "Your LSC"), 2026 editions. The short-course cut table does double duty: it is the Gold Championships floor and the ceiling for the Silver qualifier meets (Silver entry floors are printed per-meet in each Silver announcement, so there is no single Silver table to show). The LC Championships announcement prints numeric qualifying times for every individual event (its slower "Bonus Qual Time" rows are not shown, per this app's QT-only rule). Note: the Senior boys' 50 Free SCM standard is printed as 29.09 — slower than its own LCM mark — and is shown exactly as published. Links: SC cut times (PDF) · LC Championships 2026 announcement (PDF) · Championship meet requirements (PDF)
  • PC Champs — Pacific Swimming (pick PC under "Your LSC"), 2026–2027 editions. Age Group Championships: single-age columns (a 10 & Under column, then ages 11–14; there is no separate 8&U table — 8&U swimmers qualify at the 10&U standard). Senior meets are 13 & Over and come in three tiers — Senior Open, Senior 2 and the Senior Meet table. (PC's Far Westerns has its own standards and is not included.) Links: PC time standards index · Age Group Champs 2026–27 (PDF) · Senior overview SCY (PDF) · Senior overview LCM (PDF)
  • SCS Champs — Southern California Swimming (pick SCS under "Your LSC"), from SCS's official time-standards tool. Five age-group championships share the same age bands (5-10 / 11-12 / 13-14 / 15-16 / 17-18): WAG (Winter Age Group, Dec 2026), Spring Age Group Champs (Mar 2027, 14 & under), JAG, Elite Champs (14 & under) and Summer Age Group (2026 editions — the 2027 summer standards are not yet published). SCS publishes no Senior Championships table; its senior tier is the Senior Development (Sr Dev) standard. A few cells on the tool are internally inconsistent copy errors and were omitted rather than guessed. Links: SCS time standards tool · SCS Swim Guide (PDF)
  • SN Champs — Sierra Nevada Swimming (pick SN under "Your LSC"), 2026 editions. The 14 & Under SC Championships (Ted Curley Classic) uses its own approved table; the LC Championships (Bill Rose Classic) uses the LSC's 2025–2028 championship sheet — the two legitimately differ in places. Senior SC Championships shows the QT column only (its published Bonus column is excluded, per this app's rule); the Summer Sanders meet uses the SR+ cuts (Feb 2026). The 8&U 25-yard events have no app event code and are not shown. Links: LSC championship sheet 2025–2028 (PDF) · Senior SC Champs announcement (PDF) · SR+ cuts, Feb 2026 (PDF)
  • CC Champs — Central California Swimming (pick CC under "Your LSC"), 2026 editions (the Age Group table is the Nov 2024 – Aug 2026 cycle document). The 15-18 age group is a band: the table shown is its entry floor, and a published "de-qual" ceiling also applies (be no faster than the de-qual time in an event to enter it) — the ceiling is enforced by the meet, not shown as a rung. CC's two senior championships are the Juanita Allington (SC) and the TYR GO Challenge (LC), taken from their 2026 meet announcements. Links: CC time standards page · Allington 2026 announcement (PDF) · TYR GO 2026 announcement (PDF)
  • SI Champs — San Diego-Imperial Swimming (pick SI under "Your LSC"). One Age Group Championship table (2027) serves both the short-course (March) and long-course (July) championships. The Winter Age Group Championship has its own, slower table — the 2024 edition is the newest SI publishes and is still the linked official document. SI has no Senior Championship. Links: SI times page · Age Group Champs 2027 (PDF) · Winter AG Champs 2024 (PDF)
