# 🏊 Swim Cuts — Time Standards Ladder

**See where your kid's time lands** — one ladder from B to the Olympic Trials:
Motivational levels, Maryland LSC championships, Eastern Zone meets, NCSA,
Futures, Juniors, U.S. Nationals / U.S. Open, and the 2028 Olympic Trials
standards, all merged and sorted by time.

### ▶ Use it <a href="https://jameswebapp.github.io/swim-cuts/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">now</a>

⭐ **If this saved you some PDF-hunting, please star the repo** — it helps other
swim families find it. Feedback and corrections are very welcome: open an
[issue](https://github.com/jameswebapp/swim-cuts/issues) or email
**James2017yyz@gmail.com**.

No account, no install, works offline after the first visit. Times you enter
stay on your device (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded.

## Who it's for

- **Swim parents** — your kid just swam a time at a meet and you want to know,
  right there on deck: *what cut is that, and what's next?*
- **Age-group & high-school swimmers (about 10–18)** — Motivational B → AAAA,
  LSC and Zone championships, NCSA, Futures and Junior Nationals on one ladder.
- **College-age and senior swimmers (up to 22 and beyond)** — the Open tier:
  EZ Sectionals, U.S. Nationals, the U.S. Open, and the 2028 Olympic Trials
  standards at the top of the ladder.
- **Coaches** — a 5-second sanity check instead of flipping through six PDFs.

## How to use

1. Pick gender, age (age on the first day of the meet), course (SCY / SCM / LCM),
   stroke and distance.
2. Tap the big time and just type digits — `2850` becomes `28.50`,
   `12667` becomes `1:26.67`.
3. The ladder shows every standard for that event: the highest cut you made,
   the next cut and exactly how many seconds away it is.
   Too many rungs? Use the **Championships on the ladder** chips to pick which
   championship families appear — Motivational, your LSC, Zone / Sectionals,
   NCSA, ISCA, Futures / Juniors, and Nationals / Pro / Trials — or tap **All**.
   Motivational, your LSC and Zone are on by default; your picks are remembered
   on the device.
4. Add times to the session list and sort by level, or by "closest to next cut".

**On your phone:** open <a href="https://jameswebapp.github.io/swim-cuts/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the app</a>, then
- iPhone (Safari): Share → *Add to Home Screen*
- Android (Chrome): ⋮ menu → *Install app* / *Add to Home screen*

It then behaves like a native app — full screen and offline at the pool deck.

> **Coverage note:** at the local (LSC) level this app currently covers
> **Maryland, Potomac Valley (PVS), Virginia (VA), Middle Atlantic (MA), Metropolitan (MR), Niagara (NI) and Adirondack (AD)**, plus California's five LSCs — **Pacific (PC), Southern California (SCS), Sierra Nevada (SN), Central California (CC) and San Diego-Imperial (SI)** — more LSCs are being added one at a
> time. The national and Eastern Zone sets above apply to
> everyone. Two neighbors need no data of their own: **Allegheny Mountain
> (AM)** championships key off the USA Swimming Motivational standards
> already in this app (Single Age "A" times — the 15 & Over events use the
> 15-year-old "A"; "BB" for the 800/1500 Free at the LC age-group champs), so
> AM swimmers can read their championship cut right off the Motivational
> (Single Age) A rung. **West Virginia (WV)** state championships publish no
> qualifying times at all (entry-based), so there is nothing to add for WV.
> Want your LSC's championship cuts, or another meet's qualifying
> times, added? Open an [issue](https://github.com/jameswebapp/swim-cuts/issues)
> or email **James2017yyz@gmail.com** — happy to add more.

Notes:
- **MD Jr Champs** is a *band*, not a floor: your time must be slower than the
  MD Champs cut and at or under the Jr Champs bound shown in the ladder.
- **MA Silver Champs** is a band the same way: make the Silver standard, but be
  no faster than the Junior/Senior cut — that cut is the one that counts.
- **AD's short-course table** does double duty: it is the Gold Championships
  floor and the ceiling for the Silver qualifier meets (whose entry floors are
  printed per-meet, so there is no single Silver table to show).
- **NI Silver Champs** is a band under the Gold cut (one published exception:
  the 10&U 500 Free ceiling is the 11-12 Gold standard). NI Bronze has no floor
  — its ceiling is the Silver table itself — so it is not a separate rung.
- **MR AGTC (15-18)** is also a band: make the AGTC Min, but be no faster than
  the AGTC Max, which sits at the Senior Mets cut. MR Bronze Champs has no
  floor at all — its ceiling is the Silver column itself — so it is not a
  separate rung. MR's short-course age-group chart is the 2022–2023 printing,
  still linked as the standing chart for the current season.
- Each meet shows the edition closest to today going forward: if the next
  edition's standards aren't published yet, the most recent one is used.
